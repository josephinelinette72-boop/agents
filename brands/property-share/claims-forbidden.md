Marketing tokenized real estate can be marketing a security. Until a real
offering exists with a legal exemption, content must not do any of these:

1. Promise, predict, or imply returns, income, yield, appreciation, or "passive income", in any wording or number.
2. Describe shares as an investment opportunity, or invite anyone to buy, invest, reserve, pre-register, or join a waitlist for shares.
3. Suggest a real property, building, company or offering exists or is coming on a date.
4. Say or imply it is safe, risk-free, guaranteed, insured, regulated, approved, or compliant with any law or regulator.
5. Say it is audited or secure. (It has tests; it is not audited.)
6. Claim liquidity ("sell anytime", "instant exit") or a market price for shares.
7. Give tax, legal, or investment advice.
8. Compare favorably with specific competitors' returns or legal status.
9. Use testimonials, user counts, or partner names (there are none yet).
10. Target retail investors (e.g. "grow your savings", "for first-time investors").

Required: any post that shows the product in use must make clear it is on a test network.
