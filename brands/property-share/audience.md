# Audience — PropertyShare

## 1. Web3 / Solidity developers
- Care about: clean contract design, gas, security patterns (reentrancy, reward-per-share accounting), tests.
- Where: X, GitHub, Farcaster, dev newsletters.
- Useful content: explainers of a design decision, code snippets, "how we test X", lessons learned.

## 2. Proptech and real-estate operators curious about tokenization
- Care about: what tokenization actually changes, the legal wrapper (LLC/SPV), compliance, operations (rent collection, investor reporting).
- Where: LinkedIn, industry newsletters.
- Useful content: plain-language explainers, "what a token does not do", operational walkthroughs.

## 3. Curious learners
- Care about: understanding self-custody and how on-chain rent splitting works.
- Where: blog, X threads.

## Not an audience right now
Retail investors looking for returns. Do not target, address or court them in any content.
